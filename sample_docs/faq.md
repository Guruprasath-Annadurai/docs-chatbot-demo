# Little Crumb Bakery: Frequently asked questions

> FICTIONAL SAMPLE DATA. Little Crumb Bakery is not a real business. These
> documents exist only so the chatbot demo works out of the box.

### Do you have eggless cakes?
Yes. Every cake on our menu can be made eggless at no extra cost. Please tell us when you order, because eggless sponges are baked in a separate batch.

### Do you make vegan or gluten-free bread?
We bake a vegan sourdough every day. We don't have a gluten-free kitchen, so we can't promise anything is safe for people with coeliac disease.

### Do you deliver?
Yes, we deliver within 8 km of the bakery between 10 am and 7 pm. Delivery costs ₹60 and is free on orders over ₹1,000. Orders placed before 4 pm go out the same day.

### Can I pay by UPI or card?
Yes. We accept UPI, all major debit and credit cards, and cash. We don't accept cheques.

### How long do your breads stay fresh?
Our breads have no preservatives. Sourdough keeps for 3 days in a paper bag at room temperature. Soft breads and buns are best eaten within 2 days, or frozen.

### Do you take bulk or office orders?
Yes. For 30 or more items, please order at least 48 hours ahead so we can plan the bake. Bulk orders get a 10% discount.

### Can I return or exchange an item?
If something is wrong with your order, tell us within 24 hours with a photo and we'll replace it or refund you. We can't take back food that has been opened for other reasons.

### Do you run baking classes?
Yes, on the first Saturday of every month we run a 3-hour bread-making class for up to 8 people. It costs ₹2,500 per person, including all ingredients and a loaf to take home.

### Is there parking?
There is free street parking on 5th Cross Road, and two-wheeler parking right in front of the shop.
