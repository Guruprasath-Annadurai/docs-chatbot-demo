How to order a custom cake
Custom cakes need at least 3 days' notice. Send us the date, the number of guests, the flavour and any design ideas or reference photos. We'll reply with a quote within one working day.

Advance payment
We ask for a 50% advance to confirm a custom cake order. The rest is paid on pickup or delivery.

Cancellations
You can cancel a custom order up to 48 hours before the pickup time and get the advance back in full. After that, the advance is not refundable because we will already have bought ingredients and started decorating.

Flavours
Chocolate truffle, vanilla bean, red velvet, lemon and blueberry, coffee walnut, and mango (April to June only).

Writing and toppers
Simple writing on the cake is free. Printed photo toppers cost Rs 150 extra. We don't make fondant figurines.

Wedding cakes
We make tiered wedding cakes for up to 150 guests. Please book a free tasting session at least 3 weeks before the wedding.
